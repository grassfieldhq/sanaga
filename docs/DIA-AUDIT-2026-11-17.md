# Dia and Sanaga — what Dia does better, and what it takes to get there

17 November 2026. **The question:** what does the Dia browser (The Browser
Company, now part of Atlassian) do better than Sanaga 0.5.0, and what work
would bring Sanaga to that level?

**Short answer.** Dia is ahead in four places, in this order of weight:

1. **The assistant.** Dia is a browser built around a conversation that knows
   your tabs, files, history and work apps. Sanaga has two one-shot actions
   (summarise, translate) and an extension writer. This is the whole of the
   distance that matters, and most of it can be closed.
2. **The web platform.** Dia is Chromium: every Chrome Web Store extension,
   DRM video, passkeys, and no "your browser is not supported". Sanaga is
   WebKitGTK: none of the four. This cannot be closed without changing
   engine.
3. **Reach.** macOS and Windows, sync between them with a pairing code, a
   release every week with engine fixes inside 48 hours. Sanaga is Linux
   only, syncs through a folder you set up, and has no update channel.
4. **Organisation features.** Single sign-on, device policies, audit logs, a
   bug bounty, a SOC 2 report.

Sanaga is ahead where Dia does not go at all: Linux, no account, no
subscription, a model that runs on the computer, containers, per-site
settings, the mobile simulator. Section 3 lists them; they are the reason
to close gap 1 without copying Dia's model.

## How this was done, and what it is worth

- **Sanaga:** the code was read, not the README: `src/ai.rs`,
  `src/ai_cli.rs`, `src/app/intelligence.rs`, `src/app/translate.rs`,
  `src/app/plus.rs`, `src/app/buttons.rs`, `src/history.rs`, `src/sync.rs`,
  and the four earlier audits. Each "Sanaga today" line below names where
  it comes from.
- **Dia:** public sources only — its changelog (Mac 1.57.0 and Windows
  1.6.0, both of 12 November 2026), its security, plans, apps and
  for-work pages, and reviews of autumn 2026. The list is at the end.
- **Nothing was run side by side.** Dia has no Linux build, and Sanaga was
  not built in this audit (no GTK 4 / WebKitGTK 6 here). So there is **no
  measured comparison of speed or memory** in this page, only what each
  side publishes. A line marked *(reported)* comes from a secondary source
  and was not confirmed on Dia's own pages.
- The sizes in section 4 (S, M, L, XL) are estimates for one person who
  knows the code. They are not commitments.

## 1. Where Dia is ahead

### 1.1 The assistant

| What Dia does | Sanaga today | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| **A conversation.** Chat in the new tab and the command bar, "Ask on Page" in a side panel; follow-up questions; edit a message, stop and resume, queue the next one while it writes. | One request, one answer. `ai::ask` and `ask_live` send a single user message (`src/ai.rs:81`, `:117`); the panel holds one summary (`Summary`, `src/app/intelligence.rs:111`). No question can be asked about a page, no follow-up. | **Large** — the base of everything below |
| **Several tabs as context**: @-mention a tab or a whole tab group. | The page shown only (`summarize_page`, `intelligence.rs:242`). | Large |
| **Reads the whole page**, frames and shadow DOM included, in a separate process. | The top document's `<p>` elements, cut at 80,000 characters in the page and 24,000 for the model (`PAGE_TEXT_JS`, `intelligence.rs:11`; `summary_prompt`, `ai.rs:271`). Text inside frames or web components is not read. | Medium |
| **Files as context**: PDF, images, Word, Excel, PowerPoint. | None. The PDF reader shows a PDF; nothing can be asked of it. | Medium |
| **YouTube**: summary of a video in the chat. | None (`youtube_id` in `src/app/tools.rs:53` serves the floating video only). | Small |
| **History and past chats as tools**: "what was that page I read last Tuesday" — search by date, title or address; search earlier chats. | The data is there: `history.db` keeps every visit with its time, title and address (`src/history.rs:46`), and `History::list` already filters it. No model can query it, and no chat is kept. | Medium |
| **Acts on the browser**: opens, closes, groups, renames and recolours tabs from the chat; "Tidy tabs" for ten or more untouched ones. | 32 commands exist and can be run by name (`buttons::commands`, `src/app/buttons.rs:19`; `run_shortcut`, `src/app/nav.rs:726`), but only from the palette, a toolbar button or a key — never by a model. No tidy-up of tabs. | Medium |
| **Skills**: a saved prompt with chosen tools, called with `/name`; written from a description; shared in a gallery. | Nothing saved or named. The closest things are the person's own toolbar buttons (an address, a command or a script) and the extension writer. | Medium |
| **Address bar that decides**: a small model on the computer sends what is typed to the chat or to the search engine; a key forces either. | The address bar suggests history, favourites, tabs, a calculator and search shortcuts. No route to a model. | Small once a chat exists |
| **Dictation** with live transcription, going on across tab switches. | None (no speech code in `src/`). | Medium |
| **Things made by the chat**: Reports (designed documents, commented and revised in place), decks, a page saved as PDF. | "Copy" on a summary. | Medium |
| **Suggestions on the new tab**, a Morning Brief (experimental, needs Slack). | The new tab shows favourites, frequent sites and the two reports. | Small, and optional |
| **Several model makers behind one switch** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), chosen for you. | Three ways chosen by the person: Sanaga's own extract, Ollama, Claude with one's key; Claude Code tool-less (`Provider`, `ai.rs:19`). Fewer makers, but the choice is the person's — see section 3. | Small |

What Dia does **not** have, despite its reputation: an agent that clicks
through sites, shops or books. Its own security page says its agent mode
cannot go to other websites, cannot see password fields or buttons that
cannot be undone, and shows a form fill or an email draft for approval
first. Reviewers of October–November 2026 say the same *(reported)*. So
"Dia's level" is a well-informed conversation with tools, not a robot.

### 1.5 Organisations and trust

| What Dia has | Sanaga today |
|---|---|
| SOC 2 Type II report, SAML single sign-on, Chromium and Dia device policies (AI can be switched off per domain or kind of file), logs of what left the device, a HackerOne bug bounty. | An own security audit with open points (docs/SECURITY-AUDIT-2026-11-08.md: live checks C3 still open), fuzzing at every push, `SECURITY.md`. Only relevant if Sanaga is to be deployed by organisations. A managed
settings file (an administrator's `settings` that the person cannot

### 1.6 Smaller things Dia has and Sanaga lacks

Checked against `src/` (no trace found of each):

- Shortcuts the person can change.
- Tab groups inside tab groups (eight levels), pinned groups, groups named
  by themselves. (Sanaga has tree tabs when tabs are vertical.)
- Rules that send links of a site to a given profile. (Sanaga has
  containers and profiles, opened by hand.)
- Split view that remembers its layout. (Sanaga's holds two tabs:
  `split: Option<(u64, u64)>`, `src/app/mod.rs:545`.)
- "Copy address as Markdown".
- Sending a video to a television (casting); a mini player for music.
- Hand-off of a tab from a phone.

Each is S or M; none changes the picture.

## 2. Where they are level

Vertical or top tabs, pinned tabs, tab groups, split view, focus mode, tab
search and a recent-tabs switcher, floating video, sleeping tabs, profiles,
a built-in blocker of ads and trackers on by default, import from other
browsers. On these Sanaga gives nothing away.

## 4. What it takes

### The one decision to take first

Sanaga's present rule is: *a page's text only goes to a model that has no
tools*. It is right, and it is exactly what an assistant like Dia's breaks:
searching the history, opening tabs and reading mail are tools, used in the
same conversation as page text that may hold orders for the model.

To go further, the rule has to become: **the model only ever gets tools
that Sanaga itself gives it, and Sanaga decides what each may do.**

- The tools are functions in Sanaga (search the history, list the tabs,
  read a tab's text), declared to the model through the API's tool calls —
  never the tools of an assistant installed on the computer. Claude Code,
  Codex and Gemini CLI stay as they are today: Claude Code tool-less,
  the other two never given page text.
- Tools that only read are allowed. A tool that changes something (close
  tabs, fill a form, send anything) shows what it will do and waits for
  the person, every time, as the extension writer already does.
- Addresses a model writes are never opened by themselves, and never
  loaded as images (an address can carry what the model has read).
- What a tool returns from a page is marked as page content in the
  request, not mixed with the person's words.
- A private tab's rule stays: a model on the computer, or nothing.

This is Dia's own list (approved tools only, no address opened by itself,
approval before a form or a draft), and its own caveat applies: no defence
is perfect. It needs its own security review before release.

### Steps, in order

Each step is usable on its own; 1 to 3 already change what Sanaga is.

| # | Step | What is built | Where | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ask this page.** A conversation in the side panel: a question about the page, follow-ups, stop, edit and ask again, the selection as the subject ("Ask about the selection" in the page's menu). | `ask_live` takes a list of messages instead of one prompt (Claude's `messages`, Ollama's `/api/chat`); `Summary` becomes a conversation with its turns; the summary is its first question. Read frames and shadow DOM in `PAGE_TEXT_JS`; choose what to send by size instead of cutting at 24,000 characters. | `src/ai.rs`, `src/app/intelligence.rs`, `src/page.rs` (menu) | M |
| 2 | **More than one page.** `@` in the question lists the open tabs and groups; a PDF shown, a dropped file or picture, a video's subtitles can be the subject. | Text of a tab that is asleep or not started (load it, or use the Light engine `src/net.rs` + `src/html.rs`); PDF text; pictures to models that take them; a budget shared between sources. | `intelligence.rs`, `src/app/tabs.rs`, `src/net.rs` | M |
| 3 | **Skills.** A named prompt (`/name`), with the context it wants (page, selection, tabs), kept as a text file in the profile like extensions are; shown in the palette and the ✦ menu; written from a description, read before use. | A `skills/` folder, a small format, the palette's list (`tab_search`), sync of the folder. | `src/app/plus.rs`, `src/app/buttons.rs`, `src/store.rs`, `src/sync.rs` | S–M |
| 4 | **Tools that read.** The model may search the history (date, title, address), list tabs and favourites, read a tab — under the rule above. Kept conversations, searchable, in a database beside `history.db`; none kept for private tabs. | Tool calls in `ai.rs` (Claude's tool use; Ollama's, for models that have it — others keep steps 1–3), a loop with a limit of turns, the tools themselves over `History::list` and the tab list, a `chats.db`. | `src/ai.rs`, `src/history.rs`, new `src/chat.rs` | L |
| 5 | **Tools that act on the browser**, each shown and confirmed: open, close, group, rename tabs; "tidy my tabs"; run one of the 32 commands. | A typed list of actions over `run_shortcut`, a confirmation sheet that says exactly what will happen, undo through "reopen closed tab". | `src/app/nav.rs`, `src/app/groups.rs`, `src/app/dialogs.rs` | M |
| 6 | **Ask from the address bar and the new tab.** A line "Ask ✦" under the suggestions and a key that forces it; no model deciding in the person's place at first. Dictation with a speech model on the computer (whisper.cpp), as an optional package. | `src/app/toolbar.rs`, `src/app/start.rs`; a recorder through PipeWire. | S (asking) + M (dictation) |
| 7 | **Outside tools through MCP.** The person adds their own MCP servers (mail, calendar, a tracker, a company's own). Reading tools allowed per server, writing ones confirmed each time. Dia does not offer this to its users. | An MCP client (HTTP and a local program in a private folder, as `ai_cli` runs assistants), sign-in through a tab, keys in the keyring, a settings page that lists each server's tools. | new `src/mcp.rs`, `src/app/settings.rs`, `src/keyring.rs` | L |
| 8 | **Fill and draft, with approval.** "Fill this form from what I said", "draft a reply": Sanaga shows the values next to the fields; the person accepts; never a password field, never a submit. | Built on the isolated script world the password fill already uses (`src/app/accounts.rs`). | M–L |
| 9 | **Things made by the chat.** An answer saved as a page in the profile (`sanaga://notes/…`), revised by further questions, printed to PDF by the print dialog that exists. | `src/app/pages.rs` | M |

Steps 1–6 bring the assistant to Dia's level for one person on one
computer. Step 7 replaces Dia's connected apps by something more open,
without matching its list app for app: Dia's ready-made, signed-in
connections to some twenty services are a company's work (agreements with
each service, review by each), not code Sanaga can write alone.

### What stays out of reach on WebKitGTK

Chrome Web Store extensions with background workers and pop-ups, DRM
video, and being the engine sites are tested on. Three ways out, none
free:

- **Stay** on WebKitGTK and say so (today's choice): light, native, and
  these three limits for good.
- **A stand-in for extensions**: background pages run in hidden web views,
  pop-ups in small windows, the most used `chrome.*` calls rewritten in
  Sanaga. XL, never complete, and uBlock Origin's network part would still
  not work (Sanaga's own blocker covers that use).
- **Change engine** to Chromium (CEF): `src/page.rs` (2,587 lines) and
  `src/shell.rs` (2,408) rewritten, the memory advantage and the small
  package lost, an engine to rebuild and ship every week. It would make
  Sanaga a different browser.

The recommendation is to stay, and to put the work where Dia cannot
follow: Linux, no account, a model on the computer, the person's own
tools.

## 5. What not to copy

- **An account to browse.** It is the first thing Dia's critics name after
  the price.
- **Content used to improve the product by default.**
- **AI behind a counter nobody can read.** Sanaga's cost is the person's
  own key or their own computer; say so plainly in Settings.
- **A memory of the person.** Dia removed its own in September 2026
  (version 1.50) and deleted what it held. Searching the history and past
  chats, kept on the computer, does the same work and can be read and
  cleared.
- **A model choosing in the person's place** between chat and search. A
  visible line and a key are enough, and predictable.
