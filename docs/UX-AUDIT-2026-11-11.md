# Interface audit — Settings, their navigation, Intelligence, translation, extension writing

11 November 2026. **The question:** are these at the level of the best 1 %
of applications in the world?

**Before this audit: no.** The base was good — the macOS System Settings
shape, instant changes, one design language — but each part had defects
that the best products do not ship: text cut in mid-word, a search that
missed most settings, two navigations that disagreed, dead ends, missing
characters, work in progress that could not be seen or stopped.

**After it:** every defect in the tables below marked *fixed* is fixed and
was checked on the real window (French at 1280 px in the light look,
German at 820 px in the dark look). What still separates these screens from the very
best is listed at the end — some of it is real work, none of it is hidden.

How it was done: every screen captured and read as a first-time user would,
the code of each control read, the flows run end to end — the AI ones with a
stand-in model (`tools/fake-model.py`) so that progress, bars and buttons
can be seen without an account or a download.

## 1. Settings

| # | Found | State |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Text cut in mid-word, with nothing to say so**: "…ne p", "…des notification", "…les mots de pass", "…(recom". A row's text was given 55 % of the width whatever its control needed — next to a 38 px switch, 230 px stayed empty while the sentence was cut. Worse in German and at narrow widths. | Fixed: the text takes the room its control really leaves, goes on to further lines when it must, and the row grows with it (`Rows::row`). Nothing is cut in any language or width. |
| S2 | **Missing characters**: "bouton ▯", "Menu ▯ › Plus d'outils" — the ✦ and ⋮ signs are not in the interface's fonts unless the system has extra ones. | Fixed: said in words ("bouton Intelligence de la barre d'outils", "menu principal"), in the five languages. |
| S3 | **Search missed most settings.** It only knew 17 hand-written lists of words: "notifications", "traduction", "batterie", "cookies", "webkit"… found nothing. It ignored accents the wrong way ("general" did not find "Général"), showed whole blocks without saying which row matched or which category it came from. | Fixed: the index is made from what the settings really show (every row, note, menu choice), so a setting added later is found without being declared; accents and capitals are ignored; each result says its category (click it to go there), only the groups that match are shown, and the matching rows are tinted. |
| S4 | **Two navigations that disagreed.** Settings had its own ‹ › arrows; the toolbar's arrows, Alt+← and the mouse's side buttons did nothing between categories. A category had no address: it could not be linked to, bookmarked or restored. | Fixed: each category has an address (`sanaga://settings/privacy`); going to one is a step of the tab's history, so every way of going back agrees, the session restores the category, and other screens open the right one directly. |
| S5 | **Chevrons on things that are not places.** "Synchroniser maintenant", "Chercher les modèles", "Installer une extension…", "Ouvrir le dossier…" looked like pages to go to (›) but acted at once. | Fixed: a button on the right that says what it does (`Rows::action`); chevrons are left to rows that open a page. |
| S6 | **A switch that is off was invisible in the dark look**: its track had the card's colour, leaving a white dot floating. | Fixed. |
| S7 | **Contradictory help**: the memory saver said "after 6 h, 4 h or 2 h" in its row and "Balanced: after 20 minutes" in its note. | Fixed (the note no longer gives a wrong figure). |
| S8 | **The codecs card**: its buttons ran into its text, its margins were 2 px off the other cards. | Fixed: text on the full width, the command selectable, buttons under it. |
| S9 | **Opening Settings left the keyboard in the address bar** (from a new tab), so ↑ ↓ did nothing. Ctrl+F opened the page finder, useless there. | Fixed: the keys go to the settings; Ctrl+F is the settings' search. |
| S10 | **The sidebar cut its longest name** in German ("Synchronisierung und Profile"). | Fixed: as wide as its longest name asks. |
| S11 | **Screen readers** heard nothing for rows and switches (drawn by hand). | Fixed for rows, switches (as checkboxes, on or off) and the ✦ menu. |

## The gaps the first pass left, closed

The first pass ended on a list of what still separated these screens from
the very best. Each point has been dealt with since:

| Gap | Now |
|---|---|
| **The keyboard alone.** | Tab reaches every row, switch, choice, the sidebar and the toolbar, each with a focus ring; Space or Enter acts. In one of Sanaga's own pages the first Tab goes to the page, not to the toolbar. |
| **Answers arrived all at once.** | Summaries and extensions appear as the model writes them (Ollama and Claude), with **Stop** / **Cancel**. |
| **Translation was asked for, not offered.** | A page in another language offers it in a bar (**Translate**, **Never on this site**, ×; a switch in Settings); language names are lower-case inside a sentence. |
| **The summary was plain text** in a fixed panel. | Headings, lists and emphasis are drawn; the panel is resized by its edge (300–640 px). |
| **Search stopped at the Settings.** | It also finds what lives one page further (site settings, clearing data, passwords…), and each category has **Restore the defaults** (asked twice). |
| **An extension could not be tried.** | **Try on this page** runs it on the page in front of you, with a bar to keep it or drop it, before it is switched on. |

Left as it is, by choice: categories change at once, without a cross-fade.

## 5. The other pages and the menus (second pass)

What the first pass left out: history, favourites, downloads, passwords,
extensions, profiles, site settings, import, clearing data, About, the new
tab, the menus and panels, find in page, the reader, the mobile simulator,
the side panel, the tab overview. Looked at in the real window with a
filled profile, and corrected:

| # | What was wrong | What was done |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Text cut in the middle of a letter** wherever Sanaga paints a line itself (tabs, favourites bar, history rows, tiles, side panel, menus — 35 places), and the address bar for a long address. | One helper (`Fit`): a line that doesn't fit ends with "…". |
| P2 | **Panels piled up**: the main menu, the share menu, the ✦ menu, the profile menu, the site panel and others could be open together, and Escape closed only some. | One at a time (opening one closes the others); Escape closes whichever is open. |
| P3 | **One click destroyed**: "Clear history", "Delete" on a password or an extension, "Reset" in a site's settings. | A two-step button: the first press arms it ("Confirm?", red, four seconds), the second does it. |
| P4 | **Extensions page**: descriptions ran under the controls, tick boxes where Settings use switches, "more than 35 devices" (there are 120), no way to the extension writer. | Rows with wrapped text and the Settings' switch, the real count, a link to write one. |
| P5 | **A site's settings panel**: switches at uneven places, labels above their controls, a stray rule, "Reset" always there. | Rebuilt with the Settings' cards and rows; controls on one edge; it scrolls in a small window; "Reset" only when something was changed. |
| P6 | **Find in page on web pages** (`window.find`): no count, no "not found", one match marked. | The engine's own search: every match marked, "8 results" / "No results" (red), the words kept and selected next time, marks removed on closing. |
| P7 | **History lost titles.** A title that arrived after the page had loaded was never written: after a restart the visit showed its bare address; and when another tab had loaded in between, the late title made a second visit. | Late titles rename their visit, in memory and in the file (a line added at the end, safe with several windows; old versions ignore it). Covered by a test. |
| P8 | **Local files**: the tab and lists showed the whole path, the address bar its first folders only. | The file's name, then its folder ("article.html — /home/…"); lists say "File · folder". |
| P9 | **Site badges**: "F" for fr.wikipedia.org, "0" for 127.0.0.1. | The letter of the site's own name; numeric addresses keep their first digit. |
| P10 | **A band of the wrong colour** across the last row of scrolling lists (side panel, device list). | The toolkit's fade is off: it took the page's colour, and Sanaga paints these panels itself. |
| P11 | Smaller things: the address bar kept the caret and the internal address on Sanaga's own pages; a menu's label ran into its shortcut; "Copy title and link" had no icon; site-settings badges sat below their line; the import page used a sign the fonts lack ("⋯"); the new tab's tiles let their names run into one another; About did not say which engine version is in use. | Each corrected; About shows the WebKitGTK version and links to Activity. |
| P12 | **Characters the fonts cannot draw** (an empty box): "⋮" in the welcome page, in all five languages; language names in their own script ("Chinese (中文)") on a computer without those fonts. | The welcome pages say it in words; a language's own-script name is shown only where it can be drawn. A test (`src/glyphs.rs`) now reads the fonts Sanaga carries and fails if any text of the interface or the welcome pages uses a character none of them has. |
| P13 | **A favourite's right-click menu** could open or delete it, not rename it nor give its address. | **Edit…** (the favourites page, its line open) and **Copy address**. |

Checked and left as they are: the main menu (scrolls when "More tools" is
open), the profile menu, the downloads page, clearing data, profiles, the
reader, the tab overview, tab search, the favourite popover, split view.
The welcome page was read in French, and checked in the five languages
by the font test.

Still open:

- **Tab and favourites right-click menus** are the toolkit's plain menus:
  no icons, no shortcuts shown. They close on Escape and on a click in
  the page (checked).
- **A web page's own right-click menu** is drawn by GTK outside Sanaga's
  drawing: it could not be captured here, so it was not looked at.
- **Find in page** says how many matches there are, not which one is
  current ("3 of 8"): the engine does not tell.
- **History of several windows**: each window lists its own visits until
  it reloads the shared file.

## Trying it

```sh
cargo build
./target/debug/sanaga sanaga://settings/privacy     # a category by its address
tools/fake-model.py &                                # a stand-in for Ollama (same address)
./target/debug/sanaga                                # Settings › Intelligence › Ollama, then ✦
```
