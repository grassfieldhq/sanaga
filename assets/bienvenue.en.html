Welcome to Sanaga

Sanaga is a fast, frugal browser. It shows sites with WebKit, a mature web engine (the one in Safari and GNOME Web): full layout, JavaScript, videos and web apps all work.

Need to save even more? In Settings, choose the Sanaga Light engine: it shows the essentials of pages — text, headings, links, images and forms — without JavaScript or ads, using very little memory.

What Sanaga can do

  • Tabs: each tab only starts its engine when it shows a site.
  • Smart address bar: type an address or a search, Sanaga understands.
  • Reader: the page-shaped button in the address bar shows the article alone, without menus or ads, in large text with three themes (white, sepia, night).
  • Reading List: keep a page for later (menu ⋮ or Ctrl + Shift + D); it appears in the sidebar and on the start page.
  • Tab overview (four-squares button or Ctrl + Shift + O): all your tabs as thumbnails.
  • Tab groups in the sidebar (Ctrl + Shift + L), pinned tabs, tabs you can move by dragging, right-click to duplicate, mute or close the others.
  • Share (square button with an arrow): copy the link, send by email, print, create a web app that opens on its own from the applications menu.
  • Cross-site tracking prevention and a Privacy Report on the start page.
  • Translate the page, full screen (F11), page source (Ctrl + U), Web Inspector (Ctrl + Alt + I), zoom remembered per site.
  • Passwords: Sanaga offers to save them in your system's keyring and to fill them in next time; AutoFill of your details (Ctrl + Shift + F).
  • Profiles (Personal, Work…): each with its own favourites, history, passwords and cookies, in its own window.
  • Extensions: ad blocker, no autoplay, dark mode for every site, and your own scripts.
  • Sync through a shared folder: favourites, reading list and your other devices' tabs.
  • Memory Saver: unused tabs go to sleep and reload when you come back.
  • Address bar: switch to an open tab, search shortcuts (w Wikipedia, yt YouTube, m map, tr translation…) and a calculator (type 12*3).
  • Search tabs (Ctrl + Shift + A), save page (Ctrl + S), the page's QR code in Share.
  • Site permissions (camera, microphone, location, notifications) and clearing browsing data (Ctrl + Shift + Del).
  • Import your favourites from Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi or an HTML file.
  • Multilingual: français, English, español, português, Deutsch (Settings › Language).
  • Favourites and history, stored on your computer.
  • Private browsing: nothing is saved, cookies are kept apart.
  • Two engines: WebKit for full sites, Sanaga Light to read fast with very little memory.
  • Downloads to your Downloads folder.
  • Find in page and zoom.

Good to know

The WebKit engine shows sites like a classic browser. The Sanaga Light engine doesn't run JavaScript: webmail, videos and web apps are incomplete there, but news sites, encyclopedias and blogs read very well.

To get started, try Wikipedia, Hacker News or NPR text version.

Sanaga — named after the longest river in Cameroon.
